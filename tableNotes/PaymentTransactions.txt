Table: PaymentTransactions
Module: Sales
Approximate row count: 41
Description: Individual money movements in and out of a wallet. Type is Deposit or Withdraw, and FromType/ToType say whether the counterparty is a Customer, a Wallet or a Supplier.
Relationships: Joins to 'Customers' on FromCustomerId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'Invoices' on InvoiceId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'PaymentBoxes' on PaymentBoxId. Joins to 'PaymentRequests' on PaymentRequestId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'Suppliers' on ToSupplierId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN).
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Amount (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- Base (NVARCHAR(10), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- ExchangeRate (DECIMAL(18,6), NULL)
- FromCustomerId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Customers.Id)
- FromType (NVARCHAR(20), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: Customer | Wallet | Supplier
- InvoiceId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Invoices.Id)
- IsAuto (BIT, NOT NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- PaymentBoxId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PaymentBoxes.Id)
- PaymentRequestId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> PaymentRequests.Id)
- ToPaymentBoxId (BIGINT, NULL)
- ToSupplierId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Suppliers.Id)
- ToType (NVARCHAR(20), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: Customer | Wallet | Supplier
- TxCurrency (NVARCHAR(10), NULL)
- Type (NVARCHAR(20), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: Deposit | Withdraw
