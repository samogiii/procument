Table: CompanyPresets
Module: Catalog
Approximate row count: 7
Description: The issuing company identity used on generated PDFs: branding, logo, colours, bank details, SMTP and IMAP settings. A business may invoice under several company presets.
Relationships: Joined from 'CompanyPresetBankAccounts' on CompanyPresetBankAccounts.CompanyPresetId. Joined from 'CompanyPresetWarehouses' on CompanyPresetWarehouses.CompanyPresetId. Joined from 'PaymentBoxes' on PaymentBoxes.CompanyPresetId. Joined from 'PaymentRequests' on PaymentRequests.CompanyPresetId.
