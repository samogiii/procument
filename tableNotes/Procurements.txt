Table: Procurements
Also known as: procurement group, purchasing group
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 28
Description: The post-acceptance purchasing group, created one-to-one from an accepted sales order. This is the purchasing team's container of work. It has NO CustomerId column: to find the customer you must go through InvoiceId to the Invoices table.
Relationships: Joined from 'ProcurementItems' on ProcurementItems.ProcurementId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- CreatedByUserId (BIGINT, NULL)
- FinalizedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- FinalizedByUserId (BIGINT, NULL)
- InvoiceId (BIGINT, NOT NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(2000), NULL)
- ProcurementNumber (NVARCHAR(100), NOT NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL, default "Open")
  Allowed values: Open | Finalized | Cancelled
Notes: Do not confuse with the Procument table. There is no CustomerId here: reach the customer via InvoiceId -> Invoices.CustomerId.
