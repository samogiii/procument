Table: TrackNumberDocuments
Module: Shipping
Approximate row count: 12
Description: Documents and photos uploaded against an inbound track number or PO item.
Relationships: Joins to 'POItems' on POItemId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'POItemTrackNumbers' on TrackNumberId. Joins to 'Users' on UploadedByUserId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- FileName (NVARCHAR(500), NOT NULL)
- FileSizeBytes (BIGINT, NOT NULL)
- MimeType (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- OriginalFileName (NVARCHAR(500), NOT NULL)
- POItemId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> POItems.Id)
- TrackNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> POItemTrackNumbers.Id)
- UploadedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- UploadedByUserId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
