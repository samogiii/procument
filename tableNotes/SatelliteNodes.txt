Table: SatelliteNodes
Module: Infrastructure
Approximate row count: 1
Description: Satellite application instances deployed per base, used by the data sync mechanism.
Relationships: Joined from 'SyncRegistries' on SyncRegistries.SatelliteNodeId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- BaseNumber (INT, NOT NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- EndpointUrl (NVARCHAR(500), NOT NULL)
- IsActive (BIT, NOT NULL)
- LastSyncAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- ModifyAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- Name (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- PublicKey (NVARCHAR(MAX), NOT NULL)
- SharedSecret (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
