Table: PartNumberSuppliers
Module: Catalog
Approximate row count: 2483
Description: Junction table for the many-to-many relationship between parts and the suppliers that carry them.
Relationships: Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId. Joins to 'Suppliers' on SupplierId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
- SupplierId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Suppliers.Id)
