Table: RFQItems
Module: RFQ
Approximate row count: 2340
Description: The individual part lines a customer asked us to price on an RFQ. Note that Qty is a float here, not an integer.
Relationships: Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId. Joins to 'RFQs' on RFQId. Joined from 'Procument' on Procument.RFQItemId. Joined from 'QuoteItems' on QuoteItems.RFQItemId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Alt (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- IsHighlighted (BIT, NOT NULL)
- Note (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
- Priority (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Qty (FLOAT, NOT NULL)
- RFQId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> RFQs.Id)
- Unit (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
Notes: Qty is a FLOAT, not an integer.
