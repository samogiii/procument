Table: Users
Also known as: staff, experts, employees
Module: Identity
Approximate row count: 14
Description: Application user accounts. Role is one of SuperAdmin, Admin, Payment, AHM, Expert or Inventory, and controls which menus and routes the user can reach.
Relationships: Joined from 'EntityPermissions' on EntityPermissions.UserId. Joined from 'Procument' on Procument.UserId. Joined from 'Quotes' on Quotes.UserId. Joined from 'RFQs' on RFQs.UserId. Joined from 'ShipmentNotes' on ShipmentNotes.CreatedByUserId. Joined from 'Tasks' on Tasks.AssignedToUserId. Joined from 'TrackNumberDocuments' on TrackNumberDocuments.UploadedByUserId. Joined from 'TrackNumberItems' on TrackNumberItems.ReviewedByUserId. Joined from 'UserBases' on UserBases.UserId. Joined from 'UserCustomers' on UserCustomers.UserId. Joined from 'UserWarehouses' on UserWarehouses.UserId. Joined from 'WarehouseTransfers' on WarehouseTransfers.CreatedByUserId. Joined from 'WarehouseTransfers' on WarehouseTransfers.ReceivedByUserId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- Email (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- IsActive (BIT, NOT NULL)
- ModifyAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- Name (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- Password (NVARCHAR(500), NOT NULL)
- Role (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: SuperAdmin | Admin | Payment | AHM | Expert | Inventory
