Table: Warehouses
Module: Shipping
Approximate row count: 2
Description: Our own warehouses and supplier drop-off points, distinguished by the Type column.
Relationships: Joined from 'CompanyPresetWarehouses' on CompanyPresetWarehouses.WarehouseId. Joined from 'POItemTrackNumbers' on POItemTrackNumbers.WarehouseId. Joined from 'ShipmentNotes' on ShipmentNotes.WarehouseId. Joined from 'UserWarehouses' on UserWarehouses.WarehouseId. Joined from 'WarehouseTransfers' on WarehouseTransfers.FromWarehouseId. Joined from 'WarehouseTransfers' on WarehouseTransfers.ToWarehouseId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Address (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- DisplayName (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Email (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- FedexAccount (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- IsActive (BIT, NOT NULL)
- Name (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- Phone (NVARCHAR(50), NULL)
- ServicePriority (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- ShipToAddress (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Type (NVARCHAR(30), NOT NULL, default "OurWarehouse")
  Allowed values: OurWarehouse | supplier drop point
