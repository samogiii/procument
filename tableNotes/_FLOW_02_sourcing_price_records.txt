Note: Flowchart Stage 2 - Sourcing and Price Records
Type: Application Flowchart
Stage: 2 of 10
Description: How an expert finds prices before quoting. For each requested part the expert contacts suppliers and records what each one offers. These pre-acceptance offers live in the Procument table (spelled with one 'e'). Business users call them price records, sourcing records or supplier offers. Nothing has been committed to at this stage.
Flowchart:
  Expert opens an RFQItem
     |
     v
  For each supplier approached, insert a Procument row:
     RFQItemId, SupplierId, Price, Qty, Condition, LeadTime, CertName, ShippingPoint,
     Coef_1 / Coef_2 / Coef_3 markup coefficients, Type = 'Procument'
     |
     v
  Expert compares offers and picks the pricing to quote
     |
     v
  RFQ moves to 'Ready To Quote', or 'Waiting For Admin' if admin review is required
Tables involved: Procument, RFQItems, Suppliers, Users.
Key columns: Procument.RFQItemId, Procument.SupplierId, Procument.Price, Procument.FixPrice, Procument.Type, Procument.ParentProcumentId (shop records hang off a parent).
Joins: Procument.RFQItemId -> RFQItems.Id. Procument.SupplierId -> Suppliers.Id. Procument.UserId -> Users.Id (nullable). QuoteItems.ProcumentRecordId -> Procument.Id links a sourcing offer forward into the quote.
Notes: Do not confuse Procument with Procurements. Procument is pre-acceptance sourcing during the RFQ stage. Procurements is the post-acceptance purchasing group created only after the customer buys. Suppliers never quote the customer directly; they only make offers to us.
