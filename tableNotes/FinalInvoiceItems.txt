Table: FinalInvoiceItems
Module: Sales
Approximate row count: 18
Description: The shipped lines on a final invoice, including certificate names and carrier tracking detail.
Relationships: Joins to 'FinalInvoices' on FinalInvoiceId. Joins to 'InvoiceItems' on InvoiceItemId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN).
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Carrier (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CertName (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Discount (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- FinalInvoiceId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> FinalInvoices.Id)
- InvoiceItemId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> InvoiceItems.Id)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
- Qty (INT, NOT NULL)
- TotalPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- TrackNumber (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- UnitPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
