Table: FinalInvoices
Also known as: commercial invoice, shipped invoice
Module: Sales
Approximate row count: 12
Description: The commercial invoice issued once goods actually ship, also called the shipped invoice. ProformaInvoiceId points at the originating sales order in the Invoices table.
Relationships: Joins to 'Customers' on CustomerId. Joins to 'Invoices' on ProformaInvoiceId. Joined from 'FinalInvoiceItems' on FinalInvoiceItems.FinalInvoiceId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- CustomerId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Customers.Id)
- DueDate (DATETIME2, NULL)
- InvoiceNumber (NVARCHAR(100), NOT NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(2000), NULL)
- PaidDate (DATETIME2, NULL)
- ProformaInvoiceId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Invoices.Id)
- ShippingCost (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- ShippingMethod (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: Draft | Net30 | Completed | Paid
- TotalAmount (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
