Table: InvoiceItems
Also known as: Sales order lines, sales order items
Module: Sales
Approximate row count: 146
Description: Sales order lines. This is the selling side of every margin and profitability calculation.
Relationships: Joins to 'Invoices' on InvoiceId. Joins to 'QuoteItems' on QuoteItemId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joined from 'FinalInvoiceItems' on FinalInvoiceItems.InvoiceItemId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Discount (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- ExpectedDeliveryDate (DATETIME2, NULL)
- InvoiceId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Invoices.Id)
- Qty (INT, NOT NULL)
- QuoteItemId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> QuoteItems.Id)
- TotalPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- UnitPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
