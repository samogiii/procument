Table: POImportDetails
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 5
Description: A one-to-one block of shipping, banking and incoterm detail attached to a purchase order.
Relationships: Joins to 'PurchaseOrders' on PurchaseOrderId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- ABA (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- BankAccountNumber (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- BankAddress (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- BankCity (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- BankCountry (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- BankName (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CourierName (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- FedExAccount (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Incoterms (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(2000), NULL)
- PurchaseOrderId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PurchaseOrders.Id)
- ShippingMethod (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- SwiftCode (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Wirefee (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
