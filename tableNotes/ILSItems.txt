Table: ILSItems
Module: ILS/Inventory
Approximate row count: 4
Description: Stock listing items offered through the ILS channel.
Relationships: Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId. Joins to 'Procument' on ProcumentRecordId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joined from 'ILSItemSerials' on ILSItemSerials.ILSItemId. Joined from 'ILSQuoteItems' on ILSQuoteItems.ILSItemId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- AltPartNumber (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CertName (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- Description (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- LeadTime (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
- Price (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- ProcumentRecordId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Procument.Id)
- Qty (FLOAT, NOT NULL)
- TagDate (DATE, NULL)
