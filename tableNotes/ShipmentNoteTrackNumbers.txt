Table: ShipmentNoteTrackNumbers
Module: Shipping

Description: Many-to-many junction connecting an outbound shipment note to the inbound track numbers whose goods it contains. Joining through this table fans out in both directions.
Relationships: Joins to 'ShipmentNotes' on ShipmentNoteId. Joins to 'POItemTrackNumbers' on TrackNumberId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- ShipmentNoteId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> ShipmentNotes.Id)
- TrackNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> POItemTrackNumbers.Id)
Notes: Many-to-many junction: joining through it fans out in both directions. Pre-aggregate before combining with any other child table.
