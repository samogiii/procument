Table: ILSQuoteItems
Module: ILS/Inventory
Approximate row count: 4
Description: Lines on an ILS quote. ILSItemSerialId is a soft reference with no real foreign key, so the quote keeps its history even if the serial is removed.
Relationships: Joins to 'ILSItems' on ILSItemId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'ILSQuotes' on ILSQuoteId. Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- AltPartNumber (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- BasePrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- CertName (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Coef (DECIMAL(18,4), NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- ILSItemId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> ILSItems.Id)
- ILSItemSerialId (BIGINT, NULL)
- ILSQuoteId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> ILSQuotes.Id)
- LeadTime (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
- Qty (FLOAT, NOT NULL)
- SellPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- SerialNumber (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- TotalPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
