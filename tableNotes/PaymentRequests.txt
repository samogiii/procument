Table: PaymentRequests
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 11
Description: Requests to pay a purchase order, routed for approval to the payment team.
Relationships: Joins to 'CompanyPresets' on CompanyPresetId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'PurchaseOrders' on POId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joined from 'PaymentTransactions' on PaymentTransactions.PaymentRequestId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CompanyPresetId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> CompanyPresets.Id)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- IsActive (BIT, NOT NULL)
- ModifyAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- POId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> PurchaseOrders.Id)
- PRId (BIGINT, NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(50), NULL)
