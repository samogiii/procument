Table: ProcurementItems
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 69
Description: A line on the purchasing worklist, representing one part that must be bought. It carries denormalised snapshot columns copied from upstream at creation time (RfqName, QuoteNumber, QuoteUnitPrice, PartNumberName, and the Supplier fields). Those snapshots are deliberately allowed to differ from the live source rows, so read them directly rather than re-joining upstream.
Relationships: Joins to 'Suppliers' on CurrentSupplierId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'Procurements' on ProcurementId. Joined from 'POItems' on POItems.SourceProcurementItemId. Joined from 'ProcurementSupplierQuotes' on ProcurementSupplierQuotes.ProcurementItemId.
