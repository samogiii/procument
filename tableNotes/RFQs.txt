Table: RFQs
Also known as: request for quote, enquiry, request
Module: RFQ
Approximate row count: 444
Description: Request For Quote header: an incoming enquiry from a customer asking us to price a list of parts. UserId is the assigned expert. LeadTime is the customer's deadline, and a background service auto-expires overdue RFQs to 'No Quote'. This is the entry point of the whole business pipeline.
Relationships: Joins to 'Customers' on CustomerId. Joins to 'Users' on UserId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joined from 'Quotes' on Quotes.RFQId. Joined from 'RFQItems' on RFQItems.RFQId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- CustomerId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Customers.Id)
- ExType (INT, NULL)
- LeadTime (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- ModifyAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- Name (NVARCHAR(300), NOT NULL)
- NoQuoteReason (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- ReceivedDate (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- RejectionNote (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(450), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: Open | In Progress | Waiting For Admin | Ready To Quote | Sent | Accepted | Rejected | No Quote
- UserId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
