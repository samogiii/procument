Table: CustomerPayments
Module: Sales
Approximate row count: 14
Description: Proof-of-payment uploads recorded against a sales order. Sum these to work out how much a customer has actually paid.
Relationships: Joins to 'Invoices' on InvoiceId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Amount (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- FileName (NVARCHAR(500), NOT NULL)
- InvoiceId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Invoices.Id)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
