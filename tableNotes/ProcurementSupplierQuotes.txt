Table: ProcurementSupplierQuotes
Also known as: competing supplier offers, vendor offers
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 495
Description: The competing offers from different suppliers for one procurement item. The row where IsSelected equals 1 is the vendor we actually chose, and its Price is the cost used for margin calculations.
Relationships: Joins to 'ProcurementItems' on ProcurementItemId. Joins to 'Suppliers' on SupplierId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN).
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- AddedByUserId (BIGINT, NULL)
- Alt (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CertName (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- IsSelected (BIT, NOT NULL)
- LeadTime (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Note (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Price (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- ProcurementItemId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> ProcurementItems.Id)
- Qty (FLOAT, NOT NULL)
- ShippingCost (FLOAT, NULL)
- ShippingPoint (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- SortOrder (INT, NOT NULL)
- SourceProcumentRecordId (BIGINT, NULL)
- SupplierId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Suppliers.Id)
- SupplierName (NVARCHAR(300), NOT NULL)
- TagDate (DATE, NULL)
- Unit (NVARCHAR(50), NULL)
Notes: This is a one-to-many child of ProcurementItems. Collapse it with OUTER APPLY (SELECT TOP 1 ... ORDER BY Id DESC) before joining anything else, or parent totals will multiply.
