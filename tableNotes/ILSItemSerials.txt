Table: ILSItemSerials
Module: ILS/Inventory
Approximate row count: 1
Description: Serial-numbered units of stock beneath an ILS item, each with its own condition, location, price and certificate images.
Relationships: Joins to 'ILSItems' on ILSItemId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CertImageFileName (NVARCHAR(300), NULL)
- CertImageOriginalName (NVARCHAR(300), NULL)
- CertText (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- ILSItemId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> ILSItems.Id)
- LeadTime (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Location (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- PartImageFileName (NVARCHAR(300), NULL)
- PartImageOriginalName (NVARCHAR(300), NULL)
- Price (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- SerialNumber (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- TagDate (DATE, NULL)
