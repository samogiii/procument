Table: InventoryItems
Module: ILS/Inventory
Approximate row count: 12252
Description: On-hand inventory records with quantity, condition and price.
Relationships: Joins to 'Suppliers' on CompanyId. Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CompanyId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Suppliers.Id)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- Description (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
- Price (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- Qty (FLOAT, NOT NULL)
- SerialNumber (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
