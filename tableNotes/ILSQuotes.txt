Table: ILSQuotes
Module: ILS/Inventory
Approximate row count: 4
Description: A quote issued through the ILS channel, to an ILS customer.
Relationships: Joins to 'ILSCustomers' on ILSCustomerId. Joined from 'ILSQuoteItems' on ILSQuoteItems.ILSQuoteId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- BillTo (NVARCHAR(2000), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- ILSCustomerId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> ILSCustomers.Id)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(2000), NULL)
- QuoteNumber (NVARCHAR(100), NOT NULL)
- RfqReference (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- ShipTo (NVARCHAR(2000), NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)
- TotalAmount (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
