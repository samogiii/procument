Table: Alternatives
Module: Catalog
Approximate row count: 585
Description: Alternate or superseding names for a part number. One part can have many alternatives.
Relationships: Joins to 'PartNumbers' on PartNumberId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- Name (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- PartNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> PartNumbers.Id)
