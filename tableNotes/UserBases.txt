Table: UserBases
Module: Identity
Approximate row count: 5
Description: Maps a user to the bases (offices) whose data they are allowed to see. This is the main data-scoping mechanism.
Relationships: Joins to 'Users' on UserId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Base (INT, NOT NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- UserId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
