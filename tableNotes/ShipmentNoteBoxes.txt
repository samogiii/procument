Table: ShipmentNoteBoxes
Module: Shipping
Approximate row count: 16
Description: Packing boxes with weights and dimensions on an outbound shipment.
Relationships: Joins to 'ShipmentNotes' on ShipmentNoteId. Joins to 'POItemTrackNumbers' on TrackNumberId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN).
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- BoxNumber (INT, NOT NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- HeightCm (DECIMAL(10,2), NULL)
- LengthCm (DECIMAL(10,2), NULL)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- ShipmentNoteId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> ShipmentNotes.Id)
- TrackNumberId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> POItemTrackNumbers.Id)
- WeightKg (DECIMAL(10,3), NULL)
- WidthCm (DECIMAL(10,2), NULL)
