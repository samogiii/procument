Note: Flowchart Stage 9 and 10 - Final Invoice and Completion
Type: Application Flowchart
Stage: 9 and 10 of 10
Description: How the sale is billed and closed once goods have shipped. A final invoice, also called the commercial or shipped invoice, is generated from the original sales order. When the customer pays the balance, everything closes out.
Flowchart:
  Goods shipped to the customer
     |
     v
  Insert FinalInvoices row: InvoiceNumber (unique), ProformaInvoiceId pointing at the
     originating sales order in Invoices, CustomerId, TotalAmount, ShippingCost, ShippingMethod
     |
     v
  Insert FinalInvoiceItems: shipped lines carrying Condition, CertName, Carrier and TrackNumber
     |
     v
  Status Draft -> Net30 or Completed -> Paid
     |
     v
  Balance payment arrives: CustomerPayments row plus a PaymentTransactions Deposit
     |
     v
  COMPLETION
     - sales order Invoices.Status -> 'Finish'
     - PurchaseOrders.Status -> 'Completed'
     - FinalInvoices.Status -> 'Paid'
     |
     v
  Reporting: the Total-PN margin report joins InvoiceItems back through
     ProcurementItems and POItems for full part-number traceability and margin
Tables involved: FinalInvoices, FinalInvoiceItems, Invoices, InvoiceItems, CustomerPayments, PaymentTransactions, Customers, PartNumbers.
Key columns: FinalInvoices.Status, FinalInvoices.ProformaInvoiceId, FinalInvoices.TotalAmount, FinalInvoices.DueDate, FinalInvoices.PaidDate, FinalInvoiceItems.TrackNumber, FinalInvoiceItems.Carrier.
Joins: FinalInvoices.ProformaInvoiceId -> Invoices.Id. FinalInvoices.CustomerId -> Customers.Id. FinalInvoiceItems.FinalInvoiceId -> FinalInvoices.Id. FinalInvoiceItems.PartNumberId -> PartNumbers.Id. FinalInvoiceItems.InvoiceItemId -> InvoiceItems.Id.
Notes: FinalInvoices is a different table from Invoices. Invoices is the sales order taken at the start; FinalInvoices is the commercial invoice issued at the end after shipping. A question about revenue booked usually means Invoices; a question about what was actually shipped and billed means FinalInvoices.
