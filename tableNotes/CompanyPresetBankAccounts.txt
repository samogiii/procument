Module: Catalog

- AccountName (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- AccountNumber (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- BankAddress (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- BankName (NVARCHAR(300), NULL)
- BeneficiaryName (NVARCHAR(300), NULL)
- CompanyPresetId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> CompanyPresets.Id)
- SortOrder (INT, NOT NULL)
