Table: PurchaseOrders
Also known as: PO, purchase order
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 86
Description: A purchase order issued to a supplier. InvoiceId is nullable because one PO can consolidate parts for several sales orders, so joining it as an inner join silently drops rows. It carries three independent status columns: Status for the workflow, AdminApproval for sign-off, and PaymentStatus for money.
Relationships: Joins to 'Suppliers' on SupplierId. Joined from 'POImportDetails' on POImportDetails.PurchaseOrderId. Joined from 'POItems' on POItems.POId. Joined from 'PaymentRequests' on PaymentRequests.POId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- AdminApproval (NVARCHAR(20), NOT NULL, default "Pending")
  Allowed values: Pending | Approved | Rejected
- AdminApprovalAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- AdminApprovalBy (BIGINT, NULL)
- AdminApprovalNote (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- InvoiceId (BIGINT, NULL)
- PODate (DATETIME2, NULL)
- PONumber (NVARCHAR(100), NOT NULL)
- PaymentApproval (NVARCHAR(MAX), NOT NULL)
- PaymentApprovalAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- PaymentApprovalBy (BIGINT, NULL)
- PaymentApprovalNote (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- PaymentStatus (NVARCHAR(20), NOT NULL, default "NotStarted")
  Allowed values: NotStarted | in-progress values
- PaymentSubmittedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- PaymentSubmittedBy (BIGINT, NULL)
- PreferredWalletId (BIGINT, NULL)
- ProcessingFee (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- RejectionNote (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- ReturnReason (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- ReturnedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- ReturnedByUserId (BIGINT, NULL)
- Shipping (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)
  Allowed values: Waiting For Admin Approval | Waiting For Documents | Waiting For Payment | Completed | Returned | Cancelled
- Subject (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- SupplierId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Suppliers.Id)
- Tax (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- TotalAmount (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
Notes: InvoiceId is nullable: always LEFT JOIN to Invoices, or consolidated POs disappear from the result. There is no CustomerId column.
