Table: WarehouseTransfers
Module: Shipping
Approximate row count: 0
Description: Movement of stock from one of our warehouses to another.
Relationships: Joins to 'Users' on CreatedByUserId. Joins to 'Warehouses' on FromWarehouseId. Joins to 'Users' on ReceivedByUserId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'Warehouses' on ToWarehouseId. Joined from 'POItemTrackNumbers' on POItemTrackNumbers.SourceTransferId. Joined from 'WarehouseTransferItems' on WarehouseTransferItems.WarehouseTransferId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Carrier (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- CreatedByUserId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
- FromWarehouseId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Warehouses.Id)
- Notes (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- ReceivedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- ReceivedByUserId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
- Status (NVARCHAR(30), NOT NULL, default "In Transit")
- ToWarehouseId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Warehouses.Id)
- TrackNumber (NVARCHAR(200), NOT NULL)
- TransferNumber (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)
