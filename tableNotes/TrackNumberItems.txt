Table: TrackNumberItems
Module: Shipping
Approximate row count: 37
Description: Per-item receiving and inspection of an inbound parcel. Each row is one PO item inside one track number, and Status records whether it was accepted or rejected on arrival.
Relationships: Joins to 'POItems' on POItemId. Joins to 'Users' on ReviewedByUserId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'POItemTrackNumbers' on TrackNumberId. Joined from 'WarehouseTransferItems' on WarehouseTransferItems.SourceTrackNumberItemId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- ActualQty (INT, NULL)
- CertNeeded (BIT, NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- ExpectedQty (INT, NOT NULL)
- IsAvailable (BIT, NULL)
- POItemId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> POItems.Id)
- ReviewNote (NVARCHAR(1000), NULL)
- ReviewedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- ReviewedByUserId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
- Status (NVARCHAR(20), NOT NULL, default "Pending")
  Allowed values: Pending | Accepted | Rejected
- TrackNumberId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> POItemTrackNumbers.Id)
- TransferredOutQty (INT, NOT NULL)
