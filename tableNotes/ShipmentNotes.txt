Table: ShipmentNotes
Also known as: outbound shipment, SN, shipment
Module: Shipping
Approximate row count: 13
Description: OUTBOUND shipment from our warehouse to the customer. Type is the incoterm, either DDP or CPT.
Relationships: Joins to 'Users' on CreatedByUserId. Joins to 'Warehouses' on WarehouseId. Joined from 'ShipmentNoteBoxes' on ShipmentNoteBoxes.ShipmentNoteId. Joined from 'ShipmentNoteTrackNumbers' on ShipmentNoteTrackNumbers.ShipmentNoteId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- AWBNumber (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- CreatedByUserId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
- CustomsFileName (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- CustomsOriginalFileName (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- CustomsUploadedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- Destination (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- PdfFileName (NVARCHAR(500), NULL)
- SNNumber (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL)
- SONumber (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Status (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL, default "Draft")
  Allowed values: Draft | Waiting for Packing | Ship To USA | Received in Warehouse | Confirmed
- TId (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- Type (NVARCHAR(10), NOT NULL, default "DDP")
  Allowed values: DDP | CPT
- WarehouseId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Warehouses.Id)
