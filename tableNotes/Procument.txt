Table: Procument
Also known as: sourcing record, price record, supplier offer, pre-acceptance sourcing
Module: Purchasing
Approximate row count: 3470
Description: Pre-acceptance sourcing offers: the prices an expert collects from suppliers while working an RFQ, before the customer has accepted anything. WARNING: this table is spelled with one 'e' and is a completely different table from Procurements. Business users call these price records, sourcing records or supplier offers.
Relationships: Joins to 'Procument' on ParentProcumentId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joins to 'RFQItems' on RFQItemId. Joins to 'Suppliers' on SupplierId. Joins to 'Users' on UserId (nullable - use LEFT JOIN). Joined from 'CapListItems' on CapListItems.ProcumentRecordId. Joined from 'ILSItems' on ILSItems.ProcumentRecordId. Joined from 'POItems' on POItems.ProcumentId. Joined from 'Procument' on Procument.ParentProcumentId. Joined from 'QuoteItems' on QuoteItems.ProcumentRecordId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- Alt (NVARCHAR(200), NULL)
- CertName (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Coef_1 (FLOAT, NULL)
- Coef_2 (FLOAT, NULL)
- Coef_3 (FLOAT, NULL)
- Condition (NVARCHAR(100), NULL)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- FixPrice (DECIMAL(18,2), NULL)
- IsCertificated (BIT, NOT NULL)
- LeadTime (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- MyNotes (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- Note (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- ParentProcumentId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Procument.Id)
- Price (DECIMAL(18,2), NOT NULL)
- Qty (FLOAT, NOT NULL)
- RFQItemId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> RFQItems.Id)
- ShippingCost (FLOAT, NULL)
- ShippingPoint (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- SortOrder (INT, NOT NULL)
- SupplierId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Suppliers.Id)
- TagDate (DATE, NULL)
- TotalPrice (FLOAT, NULL)
- Type (NVARCHAR(50), NOT NULL, default "Procument")
- Unit (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- UnitPrice (FLOAT, NULL)
- UpdatedAt (DATETIME2, NULL)
- UserId (BIGINT, NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
Notes: Do not confuse with the Procurements table. This one is pre-acceptance sourcing; Procurements is post-acceptance purchasing.
