Table: PaymentBoxes
Also known as: wallets
Module: Sales
Approximate row count: 11
Description: Wallets. Each wallet belongs to a company preset and holds one currency. There is NO balance column: balance must be calculated from PaymentTransactions.
Relationships: Joins to 'CompanyPresets' on CompanyPresetId. Joined from 'PaymentTransactions' on PaymentTransactions.PaymentBoxId. Joined from 'WalletTransferPendings' on WalletTransferPendings.FromBoxId. Joined from 'WalletTransferPendings' on WalletTransferPendings.ToBoxId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- AccountNumber (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- BankAddress (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- BankName (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- BeneficiaryName (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
- CompanyPresetId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> CompanyPresets.Id)
- CreatedAt (DATETIME2, NOT NULL)
- Currency (NVARCHAR(10), NOT NULL)
- Name (NVARCHAR(MAX), NOT NULL)
- SwiftCode (NVARCHAR(MAX), NULL)
Notes: There is no Balance column. Balance = SUM(Deposit x ExchangeRate) - SUM(Withdraw x ExchangeRate) over PaymentTransactions for that PaymentBoxId, treating a NULL ExchangeRate as 1.
