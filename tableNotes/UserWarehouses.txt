Table: UserWarehouses
Module: Identity
Approximate row count: 2
Description: Maps a user to the warehouses they are allowed to see.
Relationships: Joins to 'Users' on UserId. Joins to 'Warehouses' on WarehouseId.
Columns:
- Id (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Primary Key)
- UserId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Users.Id)
- WarehouseId (BIGINT, NOT NULL, Foreign Key -> Warehouses.Id)
